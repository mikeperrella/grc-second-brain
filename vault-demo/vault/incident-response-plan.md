# Incident Response Plan

## Reporting

Any staff member who suspects a security incident must report it to the Security Lead within one hour of noticing it.

## Response steps

1. Contain the affected systems.
2. Find the cause and the scope.
3. Remove the cause and restore service.
4. Hold a review within ten business days and record what was learned.

## Evidence and logs

So that late-discovered incidents can still be investigated, security logs are kept for 365 days. Logs tied to an open incident are kept until it closes.

## Notifying customers

When customer data is affected, the Security Lead and Legal decide on notice and tell affected customers within 72 hours of confirming the incident. See the [[data-retention-policy]] and the [[access-control-policy]].
