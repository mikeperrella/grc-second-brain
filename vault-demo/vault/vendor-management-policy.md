# Vendor Management Policy

## Before signing

Procurement assesses every vendor that will handle customer data before a contract is signed. The assessment covers the vendor's security controls and certifications.

## Contract terms

Contracts with these vendors must require security controls at least as strong as ours and notice of a breach within 72 hours.

## Yearly review

Procurement reviews each critical vendor once a year and records the result. A vendor that fails review gets a remediation plan or is replaced.

## Leaving a vendor

When a vendor relationship ends, access is removed and the vendor confirms in writing that our data is deleted, following the [[data-retention-policy]].
