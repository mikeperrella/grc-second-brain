# Backup and Recovery Standard

## Backups

Production databases are backed up daily. Backups are encrypted and kept for 35 days.

## Restore testing

A restore test runs twice a year and the result is filed with the Systems team.

## Recovery targets

The recovery time objective is 8 hours and the recovery point objective is 24 hours.
