# MFA Enrollment Procedure

## Who enrolls

Every new staff member enrolls in multi-factor authentication during their first week.

## Steps

1. The Systems team issues a hardware security key to each administrator and an authenticator app invitation to everyone else.
2. The new starter registers the second factor and signs in once to prove it works.
3. The Systems team records the enrollment date in the access ticket.

## Lost or broken devices

A lost key or phone must be reported to the Systems team within 24 hours. Recovery needs the manager's approval and a video identity check. See the [[access-control-policy]].
