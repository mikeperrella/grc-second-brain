# MFA Enrollment Procedure

This procedure supports the [[access-control-and-mfa-policy]].

## Steps

1. The new user receives an enrollment link from the IT Service Desk on their first day.
2. The user registers an authenticator app, or a hardware key if they are an administrator.
3. The user saves their recovery codes in the company password manager.
4. The Service Desk confirms enrollment in the identity provider and closes the ticket.

## Lost devices

A user who loses their second factor contacts the Service Desk. The Service Desk verifies the
user's identity with their manager before resetting the factor.
