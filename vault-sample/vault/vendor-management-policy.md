# Vendor Management Policy

## Vendor risk assessment

Before signing a contract, Procurement assesses every vendor that will handle customer data. The
assessment covers the vendor's security controls, certifications and breach history.

## Contract requirements

Contracts with such vendors must require security controls at least as strong as Halcyon Ridge's
own, prompt breach notification, and the right to review the vendor's audit reports.

## Ongoing monitoring

Procurement reviews critical vendors every year and records the outcome. A vendor that fails
review is placed on a remediation plan or replaced.

## Offboarding

When a vendor relationship ends, access is removed and the vendor confirms deletion of Halcyon
Ridge data, following the [[data-retention-policy]].
